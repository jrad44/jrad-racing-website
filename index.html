Behind the wheel

I've been sim racing since 2023. iRacing's been the outlet for a while now. MX-5 has been one of my favorite cars to drive. It's scrappy and close racing really makes for fun enjoyable races. GT3 is equally some of my favorite racing and has caused me to grow considerably. You'll also catch me doing Endurance team races like N24, Bathurst 12hr and LeMans 24hr.

The stream is the real thing — full sessions, mistakes included. YouTube is where the highlights, onboard clips, and setup breakdowns end up for anyone who'd rather watch the short version. Building this out one race week at a time.
